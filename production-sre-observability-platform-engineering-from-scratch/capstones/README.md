# Production Engineering Capstones Suite (Capstones 1 – 7 & Final Challenge)

> **Motto**: A production engineer is forged in the fire of real distributed complexity, partial outages, conflicting evidence, and organizational scaling pressure.

---

## The Capstone Sequence

The seven capstones represent the culmination of all three tracks (Production SRE, OpenTelemetry & Observability, Platform Engineering):

```text
Capstone 1: The Resilient Production Service (Phase 236)
   └── Full multi-tier stack, OTel SDK, RED metrics, SLO definition, graceful draining.

Capstone 2: Failure-Driven SRE Challenge (Phase 237)
   └── 8 Live failure scenarios injected under load; predict, detect, mitigate, write postmortems.

Capstone 3: Kubernetes Production Platform (Phase 238)
   └── Container scheduling, cgroups quota, HPA lag, PDBs, OTel Collector DaemonSet.

Capstone 4: The Self-Service Internal Developer Platform (Phase 239)
   └── Declarative developer API (`service.yaml`) provisioning compute, database, and telemetry.

Capstone 5: Platform Product Evaluation (Phase 240)
   └── Measuring developer TTFD, cognitive load reduction, and developer satisfaction.

Capstone 6: The Major Outage War Room (Phase 241)
   └── Complex multi-system cascading SEV-1 incident with noisy, incomplete evidence.

Capstone 7: Enterprise Reliability Program (Phase 242)
   └── Designing a 6-month SRE and Observability transformation for a 30-service organization.

Final Challenge: The Staff Production Engineering Challenge (Phase 243)
   └── Full audit, architecture overhaul, and platform construction for an 80-service SaaS company.
```
