# Staff-Level Case Study Template

A staff-level case study presents an ambiguous, realistic organizational and technical scenario. It challenges the learner to navigate uncertainty, evaluate competing incentives, make explicit tradeoffs, align cross-functional partners, and design a durable outcome.

---

# Case Study [NN]: [Case Title]

> **Theme:** [Technical Strategy / Architecture Review / Product Turnaround / Incident Leadership / Migration / Cross-Team Conflict]

---

## 1. The Scenario & Setting
* **Company Context:** Size, growth stage, business model, and operational realities.
* **The Technical Landscape:** Existing systems, legacy constraints, recent architectures.
* **The Organizational Dynamics:** Team structure, reporting lines, recent leadership shifts, and historical friction.

## 2. The Trigger & Problem as Presented
* How does the crisis or request manifest?
* Quotes or escalations from executives, product managers, or engineering leads.
* What is the immediate, naive reaction from the teams involved?

## 3. The Ambiguity & Incomplete Evidence
Unlike textbook exercises, the evidence provided is messy and incomplete:
* Conflicting dashboards, anecdotal complaints, incomplete metrics.
* What is unknown and cannot be immediately measured?
* What assumptions are teams making without data?

## 4. The Stakeholder Map & Competing Incentives
* Who are the key parties?
* What are their localized rational goals?
* Where do their incentives directly clash?

## 5. The Staff Engineer's Framing
How does a staff engineer cut through the noise?
* Reframing the symptom into user and business problems.
* Identifying the highest-leverage intervention.
* Distinguishing reversible from irreversible decisions.

## 6. Options, Tradeoffs, and Strategic Choices
* Analyze at least 3 distinct architectural and operational options.
* Make tradeoffs explicit across time-to-value, complexity, and operational burden.
* State the clear recommendation and explicit non-goals.

## 7. Required Artifacts
The learner must produce concrete written artifacts to solve the case:
1. **Executive Briefing (1 Page)**: High-level alignment for directors/VPs.
2. **Technical Proposal / ADR**: Architecture, interfaces, failure modes.
3. **Execution Plan**: Milestones, risk retirement, and metrics.

## 8. Case Debrief & Expert Analysis
* What would a junior or senior engineer do here?
* What differentiates the staff-level response?
* Key takeaways and mental models reinforced.
