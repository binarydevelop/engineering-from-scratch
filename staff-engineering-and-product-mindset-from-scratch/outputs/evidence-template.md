### 1. The Context
* **Situation:** [Describe the organizational, political, and technical context]
* **Problem as initially stated:** [What was the raw complaint, request, or symptom?]

### 2. Impact & Evidence
* **Users affected:** [Who experiences the friction? Segment, role, frequency]

### 4. The Decision
* **Decision:** [State the chosen path with absolute clarity]
* **Why:** [Why this path over the viable alternatives?]
* **What did I choose NOT to do?** [List explicit non-goals and deferred scope]

### 5. Execution & Outcomes
* **Execution plan:** [Thin vertical slices, critical path, and risk retirement milestones]
* **Success metrics:** [Target outcome metric and non-negotiable guardrails]

### 6. Reflection & Leverage
* **What happened after the decision?** [Operational outcome, adoption, unexpected friction]
* **What did I miss?** [Blind spots discovered during rollout]

* **How did I create leverage?** [How did this raise the system beyond my personal output?]
* **What would a senior engineer do here?** [Contrast with localized, ticket-level response]
* **What makes this staff-level?** [Highlight the organizational clarity and durable outcome]
